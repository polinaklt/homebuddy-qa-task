# Product Questions

1. **Warning flows let the lead through.** Commercial property, mobile/modular home, "not the homeowner" and "Up to $5K" budget show a warning "Would you still like to continue?". On "Yes" the wizard continues and the answer is saved. Should these leads reach contractors?

2. **Service area on stage.** ZIP `00001` (not a real US ZIP) is treated as in-area, real ZIPs `02108` and `90210` get "no matching contractors". Is this stage configuration? Is there a check that a ZIP exists?

3. **Review links are inconsistent.** Some reviews link to company websites, one to Google Maps, two to Google knowledge panels. Which one is intended?

4. **"Trust Us From Start To Finish" numbers.** What do "Project requests" and "Estimates given" show to the user? Are the numbers real or static?

5. **No consent text on the email step.** The phone step has consent text, the email step does not. Is it needed?

6. **"Safe, Secure and Confidential" has no link.** There is no link or explanation. Should it link to the Privacy Policy?

7. **Email notification.** Should the lead get an email confirmation after submitting the form?
